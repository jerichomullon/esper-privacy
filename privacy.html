Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Esper is a personal assistant used only by its owner. This policy covers the Google account data it accesses.

What it accesses

Only the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive, and only after the owner grants permission on Google's consent screen.

How the data is used

To prepare summaries for the owner on the owner's own computer. Data is not sold, shared, rented or disclosed to anyone, and is not used for advertising.

Storage

Access credentials are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. Esper does not run a public server and does not collect data from anyone other than its owner.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Esper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
